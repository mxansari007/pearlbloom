## Honest-claims cheat sheet (read first)

**Always safe to say (true USPs):**
gold-tone finish · gold-plated look (always the word "plated") · anti-tarnish · resists tarnishing · skin-safe · hypoallergenic · lightweight · water-resistant · sweat & splash safe · faux pearl · shell pearl · CZ / American Diamond (a diamond *simulant*) · honestly priced · wide variety.

**Never say (unless literally, verifiably true):**
solid gold · real / genuine gold · "18K gold" without "plated" · real diamond · medical-grade / surgical-grade steel · thick / heavy plating · "100% tarnish-resistant" / "tarnish-proof" · waterproof / shower-safe / swim-safe (use "water-resistant") · freshwater / organic / natural pearl · "handcrafted" (unless true) · any review count, rating, MRP, discount %, or "sold" number that isn't real.

**Price language:** most pieces **under ₹499**, sets **under ₹999**. Never quote an exact price you haven't pulled from the live catalogue.

## Pain-point empathy microcopy (the 8 objections)

Drop these near product info, in an FAQ, or as reassurance lines. Each answers a real worry honestly.

1. **"Will it irritate my ears?"** → "Made for sensitive ears — skin-safe, hypoallergenic metals."
2. **"Will it turn black / green?"** → "Anti-tarnish coating helps keep the shine for longer."
3. **"Heavy earrings hurt me."** → "Lightweight enough to forget you're wearing them."
4. **"Does it look as good as the photo?"** → "Honest photos — shown on a real ear, no heavy retouching." _(needs real photos — see report P1)_
5. **"Can I wear it in the rain / gym?"** → "Water-resistant — fine for sweat, rain and everyday splashes." _(not for swimming or long soaks)_
6. **"Is it overpriced?"** → "Most pieces under ₹499. No boutique markups."
7. **"Will my order actually arrive?"** → "Secure payments, COD available, and easy returns." _(use real config)_
8. **"Will it suit daily wear?"** → "Designed for comfortable, all-day, every-day wear."

## Honest photo caption style

Goal: prove the promise without implying something untrue.

- **State the context:** "Shown on a real ear, natural daylight." / "Studio shot, true to colour."
- **If it's a customer photo, say so** — and only if it genuinely is. Never label a stock/AI/model image as a "customer photo".
- **Describe the finish honestly:** "Gold-plated finish" (not "gold"); "faux pearl"; "CZ stones (diamond simulant)".
- **Avoid:** "as seen on…", invented testimonials, or captions implying a review/rating that doesn't exist.
- **Replace AI/model hero & category images** with real product/on-ear photos (report P1). Until then, don't caption them in a way that implies a real wearer.
